01
The Reality
Prescription in hand
≠ Medicine in hand.
Obtaining a medicine seems simple: Doctor → Prescription → Pharmacy → Medicine.
But a valid prescription does not guarantee access — the patient has no way of knowing where the required medicine is actually available.
02
Rural & Semi-Urban Crisis
The problem multiplies outside major cities.
Patients face large distances between pharmacies, limited transportation, and zero centralized stock information.
Sparse pharmacy density
Limited or costly transport
No shared inventory visibility
03
The Pain Point
A medicine search becomes a physical journey.
Patients travel pharmacy-to-pharmacy, wasting money, fuel and critical time — often decoding hard-to-read handwritten prescriptions along the way.
