Privacy & Safety Philosophy
Collects only what is necessary. MediBridge is an accessibility platform, not a doctor — it does not substitute medicines or make clinical decisions.
AI handles efficiency.
Humans handle ambiguity.
◆Minimum-viable data collection
◆No clinical substitution or prescribing
◆Human escalation on low AI confidence
◆Prescriptions treated as sensitive PHI
